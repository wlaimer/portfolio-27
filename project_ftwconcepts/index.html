Grub Slippers, 2023

A one-week study: a maggot-inspired form in memory foam and string, photographed and developed with AI visualization. The rapid prototyping workflow was new then. It bridged physical and digital space extremely fast, from rough material experiment to finished product visual, and opened up completely new ways of working. Today it is standard practice.
