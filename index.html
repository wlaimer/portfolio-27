Practice

Using design as a practical and epistemic tool: questioning assumptions, considering human and non-human systems, and testing through multisensory perspectives, athlete data, and advanced prototyping.

Interested in how performance, aesthetics, ecology, and materialization shape what artifacts can do, communicate, and become.
