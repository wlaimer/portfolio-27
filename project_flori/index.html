07 / 2021

Medication
Trolley

A semi-autonomous trolley for future clinics that supplies medication sustainably and efficiently while reducing the workload of care staff.

Year
2021
Context
Salzburg University of Applied Sciences
Duration
Six months
Practice
Bachelor thesis, Industrial design, Empirical study, User and market analysis, Corporate identity, Business plan, Digital and physical prototyping

Concept

Care staff work under high physical and psychological strain. FLORI is an autonomous trolley that takes over time-consuming logistics, leaving more time for patients.

Based on theoretical analysis, quantitative and qualitative research, and a functional model, it organizes care materials in four clear zones within a closed, easy-to-clean steel form.

Features

Autonomous stock checks, refilling, recharging, and waste disposal. A connected logistics system reorders supplies, and a fold-away touch display gives access to the hospital system.

Key benefits

For care: more resources for patient care and more joy at work. For administration: cost savings and data transparency.
