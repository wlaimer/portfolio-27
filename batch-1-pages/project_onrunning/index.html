Cloudboom Volt Pro

Developed in close collaboration with elite athletes, whose perspective shaped the process from the beginning, with an uncompromising focus on one specific race.

Internship 2025

Developed multiple future product lines for performance running with technical innovations built in, and made the concepts tangible through early prototypes, material experiments, and an internal exhibition.

Created a framework that anchors products to specific running sensations and links geometries and technologies to new product generations, combining features from different performance requirements into coherent propositions. Introduced AI-driven digital workflows.
